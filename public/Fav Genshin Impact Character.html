Genshin Impact: My Favorite Characters

1. Arlechino


A 5-star Pyro character known for her high damage output and unique playstyle. I personally like her cause she is very easy to use. I love her character design and her lore. I have a deep sense of respect for her. She's really really deserving of title "Father".

2. Nahida


A 5-star Dendro character known for her support capabilities and unique team composition. She's Dendro Archon also known as "Goddess of Wisdom". I really love the characters who represent wisdom and knowledge. They remind me that wisdom is not information but rather the ability to apply information wisely to solve problems. And Nahida is just a cutie with wierd sense of humour.

3. Kazuha


A 5-star Anemo character known for his support and grouping abilities. Since release he has been my comfort character. His skills are so good his a universal character fitting in almost all team compositions. He is the first character I ever saved for and C1.
